Warranty, Delivery & Returns

Honest, simple terms for every appliance you buy from us. If something is not clear, just ask on WhatsApp.

Warranty

🛡️ New appliances

Warranty varies by brand and product. Refrigerator compressors commonly carry 3–11 years, TVs 1 year, washing machine motors 2+ years. The exact warranty is shown on each product page, and you can confirm on WhatsApp before you pay.

✓ Second-hand guarantee

Every refurbished unit includes a 30-day service guarantee. If a serviced fault appears within 30 days, we repair or replace it at no charge.

🔧 Claiming service

Message us on WhatsApp with your model number and the issue. Inside the valley we arrange a visit, or bring the unit to our Samakhushi store and our technicians will handle it.

Delivery & installation

🚚 Inside the valley

Free delivery and installation inside Kathmandu valley. We unbox, install and demo the appliance before we leave.

📰 Across Nepal

Shipping to other districts is arranged at cost via trusted couriers, with the warranty intact.

🔗 Exchange pickup

When you exchange an old appliance, we collect it during delivery. Outside the valley, bring it to the store or ask about courier options.

Returns & payment

↺ Returns

A product that arrives defective is exchanged or repaired at no charge. Always test at delivery before installation. Once installed and used, brand warranty terms apply for service.

💲 Payment methods

Cash on delivery, bank transfer, eSewa, Khalti and card are accepted. EMI is available on selected products — confirm eligibility by model code.

💰 Prices

All prices are MRP in Nepali Rupees and include delivery inside the valley. Prices may change without notice; the price we quote on WhatsApp is final for that day.
