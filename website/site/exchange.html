Exchange FAQ

How is cashback decided?

By condition (working / repairable / scrap), age and model demand. We always give an honest quote based on a photo.

Is the quote deducted upfront?

Yes. The exchange value is adjusted against your new appliance price at the store — no waiting for refunds.

Do you pick up old units?

Inside Kathmandu valley we arrange pickup with your new delivery. Outside the valley, bring the unit to the store or ask us.

Can I exchange and use EMI?

Yes. Exchange cashback is deducted first, then EMI can be arranged on the remaining amount.
